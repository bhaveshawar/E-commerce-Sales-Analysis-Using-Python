# E-commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to uncover trends in sales, profit, product categories, sub-categories, and customer segments. The analysis is performed using Python in a Jupyter Notebook to generate meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze sales and profit performance across different time periods, product categories, and customer segments in order to identify key revenue drivers, profitability patterns, and areas for business improvement.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas  
- NumPy  
- Matplotlib / Plotly  
- Jupyter Notebook  

---

## ❓ Business Questions Addressed
- What are the monthly sales trends and which months perform best and worst?
- Which product categories and sub-categories generate the highest and lowest sales?
- How does profit vary across months, categories, and sub-categories?
- Which customer segments contribute the most to sales and profit?
- What is the relationship between sales and profit across the business?

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Sales and profit show clear seasonal trends, with stronger performance toward the end of the year.
- Technology and Office Supplies emerge as the primary profit-driving categories.
- Certain sub-categories generate low or negative profit, indicating margin inefficiencies.
- The Consumer segment contributes the highest sales and profit.
- High sales do not always result in high profit, highlighting the importance of profit-focused analysis.

---

## ✅ Conclusion
This project demonstrates how e-commerce sales data can be transformed into actionable business insights. The findings help identify profit drivers, optimize underperforming areas, and support strategic decisions aimed at improving overall sales and profitability.
